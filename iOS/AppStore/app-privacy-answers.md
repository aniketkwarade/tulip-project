# App Privacy answers

Use these as the baseline only after confirming the production build has no additional analytics or SDK collection.

## Tracking

- Does this app use data for tracking? **No**
- Does this app display third-party advertising? **No**

## Data collected

- Does this app or its third-party partners collect data from the app? **No**

The native Contact action opens the system email app. TULIP receives correspondence only after the user chooses to send it outside the app. The native app contains no contact submission endpoint, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or account system.

## Data not collected by the app

- Search history stored locally on the device
- Analyse navigation history stored locally on the device
- My Footprint answers and estimates stored locally on the device
- Advertising identifiers
- Precise or coarse location
- Contacts, photos, health, financial, payment, or biometric data
- Product-interaction analytics, diagnostics, or crash telemetry sent by TULIP

Apple may independently collect App Store and system diagnostics under Apple’s policies; those are not collected by TULIP.
