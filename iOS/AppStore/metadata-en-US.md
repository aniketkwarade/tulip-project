## Description

TULIP turns climate and environmental systems into an explorable map.

Move through a living sphere of connected issues, select a node to reveal its relationships, and inspect what triggers it, what it affects, how it is measured, and which sources monitor it.

With TULIP you can:

- Explore connected environmental causes and effects
- Compare impacts from food, energy, travel, industry, and other activities
- Inspect urgency scores, monitoring methods, and scientific sources
- Estimate your carbon, water, land, and material footprints
- Follow relationships across atmosphere, oceans, ecosystems, economies, and society

TULIP combines public datasets, institutional monitoring, reported disclosures, and modeled relationships. Not every connection has the same certainty, and TULIP is not a substitute for primary scientific institutions.

## Review notes

TULIP requires no account and has no purchases, advertising, or tracking.

The app’s primary experience is bundled in the binary and does not depend on a remote website. On Explore, drag the sphere and tap a node once to reveal connected nodes; tap it a second time to open its Analyse inspector. The Analyse tab supports swipe-up expansion and swipe-down collapse. Activity Impacts compares categories and actions. My Footprint is an interactive 11-question estimator for carbon, water, land, and material footprints. Confirmed controls provide native haptic feedback, the last active tab is restored natively, and Share TULIP opens the system share sheet.

Contact opens the system email app; TULIP receives information only if the user chooses to send the message. Scientific-source links intentionally open in Safari.

No demo account is required.
