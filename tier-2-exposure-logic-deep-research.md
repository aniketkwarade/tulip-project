# LostPlanet Tier 2 Deep Research

## Objective

Research Tier 2 nodes for **additive exposure logic**.

This tier is for Earth-system, hazard, ecological, and boundary-heavy nodes where the same product slots still matter, but the framing must adapt:

- `Hidden Cost` means hidden human burden, delayed loss, unpriced exposure, or undercounted vulnerability
- `Who Pays` means burden distribution
- `Physical Limit` is the main planetary addition
- `Default Driver` means the condition that worsens exposure or slows response, not a fake market cause for a natural process
- `System Levers` means resilience, protection, planning, monitoring, and governance levers

## Non-Duplication Rule

Do **not** repeat the existing hazard description, direct harms, ecological pathway, or affected-population summary.

This tier should add:

- what exposure is not being counted
- who gets hit first or hardest
- what threshold, sink, or tolerance matters most
- what governance or infrastructure condition keeps the burden high
- what structural response reduces exposure

If a line mainly says “this phenomenon is dangerous” or “nature is stressed,” drop it.

## Product Structure To Support

### Impact on Humans

Keep existing content. Add only:

- `Hidden Cost`
- `Who Pays`

### Impact on Planet

Keep existing content. Add only:

- `Physical Limit`

### What Can Be Done

Add:

- `Default Driver`
- `System Levers`

Important:

- `Default Driver` is about exposure persistence, response failure, land-use patterns, infrastructure mismatch, monitoring gaps, or governance lag.
- `System Levers` must be system-level response levers, not vague calls for awareness.

## Output Format Per Node

For each node, return:

### `[node_id]`

- `Research confidence:` `High`, `Medium`, or `Emerging`
- `Hidden Cost:` max 2 sentences
- `Why this is not duplicative:` 1 sentence
- `Who Pays:` max 2 sentences
- `Why this is not duplicative:` 1 sentence
- `Physical Limit:` max 2 sentences
- `Why this is not duplicative:` 1 sentence
- `Default Driver:` max 2 sentences
- `Why this belongs in What Can Be Done:` 1 sentence
- `System Levers:` 3 to 5 flat bullets
- `Key evidence:` 3 to 6 bullets with source, date if available, factual takeaway, URL
