# Current-harm reassessments, version 1

These are internally authored estimates, not measured global harm totals. They use the confirmed evidence standard and fixed benchmark policy. Existing scores and rejected ordinal ranges are never numerical inputs.

Each authored row states its human outcome, harmed-population estimate and mean annual-equivalent health/function loss; its ecological outcome, allocated affected area and mean attributable condition deficit; and its attribution and overlap boundaries. The source material is context unless a separately identified source-native endpoint is used. All unsupported quantities and transfers remain visible assumptions. A source citation does not validate an assumed global amount.

Human quantity = estimated people actually experiencing the named loss × mean attributable health/function loss-years per affected person. An exposed or dependent population is never substituted directly. Mortality lifetime loss can exceed one year per death; for services the amount includes duration and reduction of function. Mixed losses use one consolidated per-person equivalent to avoid adding health and service harm twice.

Nature quantity = allocated non-overlapping affected area × mean attributable present condition deficit. This is an internally estimated integrated condition profile, not a measured six-class SEEA profile. Exposure, anomaly intensity, occurrence counts and converted-area flows are context, never substitutes for this quantity. No recovery-duration or history multiplier is applied.

The amount fields are order-of-magnitude judgments. They are not independently calibrated measurements. Missing compatible impact models, local aggregation, counterfactuals and uncertainty remain limitations; no invented confidence interval is produced. Estimates can be replaced by compatible measurements or model outputs in a subsequent version without changing the fixed benchmark mapping.

The same current assessment cut-off is 28 September 2026. Annual human amounts describe estimated current annual-equivalent consequences, with older source windows and their lag retained as context. Ecological amounts describe the current damage stock. This is not a 2027–2036 forecast. All annual-equivalent and current-stock transfers are authored assumptions.

No rarity bonus is automatic. It requires a separately documented rare asset and irreversible attributable loss; temporary bleaching, unusual weather and long recovery alone do not establish eligibility. Asset definitions must be held fixed between assessments, rather than selected to inflate a fraction.

Future nodes require their own authored row, bound definition and evidence review. Publication never fills a missing row with a template or old score. Rollout is atomic across all published issue nodes; `TULIP Legacy Score` is a separately archived exact rollback dataset.
