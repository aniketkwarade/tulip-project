# Ocean, Atmosphere, and Energy Relationship Batch 02

This pass replaces broad generated expansion around the three largest remaining
relationship queues with relationship-specific evidence and bounded wording.

## Claim Rules

- `curated_direct` is reserved for a source-supported direction and mechanism.
- `curated_local` is used for conditional, regional, multi-step, or operational
  chains where a one-step universal claim would overstate the evidence.
- Natural climate modes are not represented as products of carbon emissions or
  global temperature. Their authored edges describe observed modulation,
  teleconnection, or covariance.
- Reviewed nodes lose their generic expansion edges only after retaining at
  least three authored relationships.
- Family and peer references remain non-causal discovery context. They are not
  promoted merely to improve the headline count.

## Ocean

Direct mechanisms cover carbon uptake and acidification, warming and oxygen
loss, stratification and ventilation, shell calcification, hypoxia, bloom-driven
closures, and coastal surge impacts. PDO, Atlantic variability, Atlantic
Nino/Nina, and IOD edges use observed-modulation or teleconnection wording.

Primary evidence bundles:

- IPCC AR6 WGII Chapter 3: ocean physical, chemical, ecological, and fishery pathways
- IPCC AR6 WGI Chapters 5 and 9: carbon cycle, stratification, circulation, and oxygen
- NOAA Ocean Acidification and hypoxia programs
- NOAA PDO monitoring and the NOAA-hosted PDO/marine-heatwave study
- NOAA CPC IOD monitoring
- Nature Climate Change observations of Antarctic Bottom Water decline

## Atmosphere

The pass separates forced responses from natural variability. Warming-driven
hail redistribution and aerosol-forcing mechanisms can be direct. MJO, NAO, AO,
PNA, SAM, and QBO edges are tied to monitored circulation, rainfall, stratospheric,
or teleconnection behavior and do not imply anthropogenic phase control.

Primary evidence bundles:

- NOAA Climate Prediction Center teleconnection and oscillation monitoring
- NASA QBO monitoring and NASA GISS QBO-MJO synthesis
- IPCC AR6 WGI Chapters 6, 7, and 11
- Nature Climate Change hail-hazard research
- Nature Communications review of atmospheric blocking

## Energy

Direct mechanisms cover electricity load, transformer constraints, fossil
combustion, industrial process emissions, semiconductor water and emissions
footprints, and backup generation. Price, curtailment, reliability, and public
health edges are local where market design, dispatch, geography, or household
conditions mediate the outcome.

Primary evidence bundles:

- IEA Electricity 2026 and Building the Future Transmission Grid
- US Department of Energy transformer supply-chain assessments
- IEA Energy and AI and the LBNL data-center energy report
- IEA steel, cement, and critical-mineral transition reports
- US EPA eGRID and US EIA natural-gas emissions material
- NIST semiconductor environmental assessment
- WHO heat and health guidance

The pre-pass family queues were ocean 313, atmosphere 271, and energy 263.
After replacing researched expansions, the remaining queues contain only
taxonomic family references and non-causal peer context. Exact post-pass counts
are regenerated in `tmp-edge-promotion-docket.md`.
