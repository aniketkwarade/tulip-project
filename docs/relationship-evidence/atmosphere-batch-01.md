# Atmosphere Relationship Evidence: Batch 01

Reviewed: 2026-07-15

## Scope

This batch replaces blanket atmosphere expansion topology around:

- Atmospheric moisture amplification
- Atmospheric evaporative demand
- Lightning ignition under fire-weather conditions
- Black-carbon darkening of snow
- Climate pressure on surface ozone
- Smoke exposure burden
- Compound day-night heat extremes
- Nocturnal heat stress
- Persistent atmospheric blocking

Rossby-wave persistence and marine low-cloud decline received researched local links, but their remaining generated neighborhoods are held for the next atmosphere batch.

## Promotion Standard

- `curated_direct`: the source supports the stated direction and mechanism.
- `curated_local`: the source supports a bounded multi-step mechanism, with the intermediate step stated in the edge copy.
- `family_supported`: retained only where current evidence supports context rather than a causal claim.
- Removed: blanket expansion relationships that jumped directly to food, migration, shipping, infrastructure, or other broad abstractions without an intermediate mechanism.
