# Phenomenon Authenticity Policy

## Purpose

This policy prevents a real but analytically named concept from being presented as a formally established phenomenon. It is a release gate for nodes and is separate from the defensibility of the relationships between them.

## Release Gate

Every user-facing node must have:

- at least one official, primary, or peer-reviewed source URL;
- an explicit authenticity classification;
- node-specific evidence when presented as a reviewed phenomenon or indicator;
- a reviewed anchor when its evidence is inherited from a research family; and
- inspector wording that discloses when the exact display phrase is not a validated standardized term.

The regression audit fails when any of these conditions is absent. Generated nodes cannot be classified as reviewed phenomena.

## Authenticity Classes

| Class | Meaning | User-facing treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reviewed phenomenon | The phenomenon name was reviewed against node-specific sources. | Present normally with its sources. |
| Reviewed operational indicator | A measurable operational indicator was reviewed against node-specific sources. | Present normally and retain its metric context. |
| Source-backed operational concept | The underlying subject has relevant evidence and the label has been manually normalized, but the phrase is not claimed as a formal indicator. | Show the operational-concept disclosure. |
| Source-backed concept label | The underlying subject is supported through a reviewed anchor, but the exact display phrase is awaiting validation. | Show the concept-label disclosure and retain it in the exact-term verification docket. |

## Stopping Rule

Relationship refinement is not an open-ended prerequisite for release. The platform is within the defensible boundary when:

- the authenticity release gate has zero blockers;
- no modeled or dangling relationship has returned;
- every relationship states its evidence mode and uncertainty honestly; and
- unresolved exact-term validations remain visible as a finite audit queue rather than being represented as settled terminology.

New nodes must satisfy this policy before they enter user-facing discovery. Existing source-backed concept labels may remain discoverable while their exact terminology is verified because the inspector explicitly limits the claim being made.
