# Platform Expansion Batch 03

This pass replaces generic expansion residue around high-priority water, biosphere,
transport, agriculture, cryosphere, governance, and carbon-cycle nodes with narrower
physical, ecological, operational, and institutional pathways.

## Promotion Rules

- `curated_direct` is reserved for a source-backed one-step mechanism with high confidence.
- `curated_local` is used for sourced but conditional, regional, multi-driver, market, or governance pathways.
- `family_supported` remains non-causal context or an unreviewed expansion candidate.
- Reviewed nodes lose generic inbound and outbound expansion edges only after a researched local neighborhood exists.
- Every node remains discoverable and must retain at least three active relationships.

## Evidence Bundles

- Water: IPCC AR6 WGII Chapter 4, USGS groundwater depletion, and EPA drinking-water resilience.
- Biosphere: IPCC AR6 WGII Chapters 2 and 3, and FAO soil-erosion evidence.
- Transport: FHWA climate-resilience and asset-risk material, plus FAA severe-weather operations.
- Agriculture: IPCC AR6 WGII Chapter 5, FAO soil evidence, and ILO heat-exposure evidence.
- Cryosphere: IPCC AR6 WGI Chapter 9, IPCC polar-regions evidence, and NSIDC permafrost material.
- Governance: IPCC AR6 WGII Chapter 17 and Technical Summary, plus WMO early-warning material.
- Carbon cycle: Global Carbon Budget 2025, IPCC AR6 WGI Chapter 5, and NOAA blue-carbon evidence.

## Regression Contract

`npm run audit:defensibility-regressions` rejects duplicate or dangling edges, nodes
below degree three, uncited direct claims, the return of modeled buckets, excessive
family-supported growth, or a material loss of graph density.
