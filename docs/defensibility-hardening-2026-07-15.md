# Defensibility Hardening

Date: 2026-07-15

## Standard

A node is strong enough for user-facing discovery when:

- it has node-specific calibration, not only family inheritance
- its inspector exposes direct bundles or clearly labeled support context
- its most common neighboring edges are curated or explicitly softened as modeled
- surprising cross-domain edges are suppressed instead of left to procedural chance

## This Pass

- Promoted high-risk `undocumented` nodes into direct `official_registry_link` calibration.
- Reduced weak node states by converting high-impact agriculture, water, biodiversity, transport, and industrial nodes from inherited or undocumented status into node-specific calibration.
- Added more curated neighboring edges around agriculture, water, shipping, aviation, cooling, and hydropower pathways.
- Expanded attachment coverage so newly rehabilitated atmosphere, biodiversity, and infrastructure nodes now show bundle-backed support in the inspector.
- Made discovery and analyze ranking evidence-aware so stronger evidence surfaces earlier than weaker procedural structure.
- Tightened inspector wording so generated edges read as provisional modeled pathways, not settled causal facts.
