# A source timebase is not a harm timebase

Prepared 27 September 2026. This audit makes no rating changes. Its [machine-readable 354-node inventory](../../artifacts/tulip-consequence-rule-audit-2026-09-27/timebase-conversion-audit.json) is built by [the audit script](../../scripts/audit-tulip-outcome-timebase.mjs) from the current public assessment registry and the reviewed route inventory.

The existing records describe **150 latest-state stocks, 60 annualized flows, 75 event distributions and 69 period-prevalence summaries**. All 27 response nodes remain outside danger scoring. These categories tell us how a source was summarized. They do **not** establish a comparable consequence burden: the route inventory still has **zero completed typed consequence endpoints** across the 354 issue nodes. The two source-native pilots are experimental endpoint inputs outside those complete catalogue assessments.

Four examples show the conversion that is missing:

| Node | Source timebase | What the source can say | What the score still needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Global Temperature | Latest-state stock | Degrees of warming relative to its stated reference | People and ecosystem consequences attributable to that state in a declared assessment period; degrees alone are not harm. |
| Deforestation | Annualized flow | FAO's gross forest-conversion area averaged over 2015–2025 | Ecosystem condition and persistence at the converted sites, plus attributable human consequences; hectares alone are not a nature score. |
| Wet-Bulb Heat | Event distribution | The frequency and intensity of dangerous heat-and-humidity episodes | Consequences for the people and ecosystems affected by compatible events, without repeatedly counting the same outcome. |
| Air Pollution Health Burden | Period prevalence in the v6 registry | WHO separately reports a single-year attributable DALY burden | A reviewed node boundary and a compatible current assessment; the WHO joint particulate-matter result excludes some outcomes named by the node. |

The next method candidate should compare **current attributable consequences**, not source-history length, physical anomaly, exposure or event count. To make that operational, each endpoint needs a declared assessment year or representative period; a rule for translating stock, flow, prevalence or events into a common annual consequence basis where justified; the source's unit and uncertainty; and an overlap-safe path to the people or nature component. A WHO DALY total already includes future healthy years lost because of deaths in its reporting year: multiplying it again by an assumed duration would count loss twice. A decade-average forest-conversion flow is neither a 2025 observation nor the damaged forest stock in 2025.

**Unresolved method decision:** annualizing inputs does not by itself make different kinds of harm interchangeable. Health, essential-service deprivation and ecosystem condition require their own fixed, source-checked anchors and a declared within-domain aggregation rule. Evidence from different years also needs a recency rule or a justified model update; calling an old value “current” does not update it. The public v6 decimals remain visible while this consequence-level basis is developed and tested.
