## What TULIP is asking for

TULIP explains 381 connected climate topics. We intend to compare current harm using the newest reliable evidence available for each node, on one 1–10 scale with equal emphasis on people and nature. Evidence periods may differ but must be dated and normalized consistently. A separate 2027–2036 outlook under SSP2-4.5 reports direction without changing the grade. The catalogue contains 354 issue nodes and 27 interventions; interventions are excluded from danger ratings. The current implementation has no approved numerical harm ratings.

After the internal 20-node pilot is complete, commission a bounded **challenge and reproduction review**. Ask for:

1. Attempt to reproduce selected internal classifications and calculations from the cited evidence.
2. Identify unsupported assumptions, overlap, bias, scenario mismatch and misleading precision.
3. Test whether the fixed rubric anchors behave consistently across node classes.
4. Recommend corrections with reasons, while leaving TULIP responsible for product weights and final policy choices.

The output should explicitly say which claims it supports. A statistics review cannot certify ecology; a domain review cannot substitute for index validation. Publication peer review of an input study is useful evidence, not approval of TULIP's transformations.

## Draft A — statistical/index review enquiry

Subject: Independent feasibility review for a climate-harm comparison score

Hello,

I am developing TULIP, a platform that explains connected climate issues. We need an independent assessment of a proposed 1–10 rating for current harm using the newest reliable evidence available for each node, with equal weight for consequences to people and ecosystems. Evidence periods are dated and normalized consistently. We report a separate 2027–2036 outlook under SSP2-4.5.

We have not approved numerical ratings. I would like to commission a bounded feasibility review of the construct, normalization, overlap between related issues, uncertainty and validation strategy. We have a draft protocol, eight case dossiers and a catalogue evidence inventory ready to share.

Could you advise whether this fits your remit, who could undertake it, and the likely scope, fee and availability? We would welcome a critical opinion, including a finding that parts of the proposed score are not supportable. Please also identify any conflicts or independence limitations.

Thank you,
Aniket

For JRC, replace the commissioning sentence with an enquiry about eligibility for COIN methodological feedback or an appropriate referral; no assumption of paid or free service.
