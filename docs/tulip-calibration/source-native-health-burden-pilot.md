# Source-native health-burden pilot: air pollution

Prepared 27 September 2026. This is an input-method pilot, **not a replacement TULIP rating**.

The [WHO indicator definition](https://www.who.int/data/gho/data/indicators/indicator-details/GHO/ambient-air-pollution-attributable-dalys) describes a modeled **joint ambient-and-household** burden. Its DALY measure combines years of life lost from premature death with healthy years lost to illness for the included causes. WHO warns that separately estimated ambient and household burdens may overlap; their joint estimate is designed to avoid that double count. A DALY is a health-burden unit, not a count of exposed people or a TULIP score.

The newer [WHO World health statistics 2026 report](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240122482), section 1.4.4 on printed page 27, gives a **2021** joint household-and-ambient particulate-matter burden of **184 million modeled DALYs**, with a **95% uncertainty interval of 142–228 million**. The [source transcription](../../artifacts/tulip-consequence-rule-audit-2026-09-27/who-joint-air-pollution-dalys-2021-report.json) records that these are **rounded to millions**, not precise counts. WHO includes acute lower respiratory infections, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, ischemic heart disease, lung cancer and stroke. The report cites WHO's joint-air-pollution Global Health Observatory database as its source.

The [reproducible API retrieval](../../scripts/fetch-who-joint-air-pollution-dalys.mjs) still returns a **2019** global, both-sexes, all-included-causes `AIR_60` row: [181,410,734 modeled DALYs, 140,758,967–225,173,575](../../artifacts/tulip-consequence-rule-audit-2026-09-27/who-joint-air-pollution-dalys-row.json). That is the latest *matching row in that API query*, but it is **not the latest matching WHO estimate found**. The 2021 report summary is now the preferred recency example for this particular joint-health endpoint. Its rounding and report-level provenance must remain visible. Neither year can be represented as a 2026 observation.

This endpoint preserves burden magnitude before any 1–10 transformation. It is more informative about total health loss than applying the old ordinal severity/reach/duration bins to the [WHO 2021 death-only row](../../artifacts/tulip-consequence-rule-audit-2026-09-27/who-air-pollution-mortality-row.json). **Do not add DALYs and deaths**, even when both refer to 2021: DALYs already include fatal loss. Do not add the 2019 and 2021 DALY totals or treat them as uncertainty endpoints; they are different assessment years. This health endpoint also omits ambient ozone and other outcomes in the [node's broader definition](../../src/data.js), as well as nature and services. It therefore does not validate the full Air Pollution Health Burden node or its current public score.

A [future-node admission audit](./future-node-admission-example-air-pollution.md) compares this WHO endpoint with the newer 2023 State of Global Air evidence. The broader source does not make a death-only or pollutant-specific result a complete, overlap-safe health-loss estimate. Its exact node coverage must be reviewed before numerical use.

The [counterfactual identity correction](./counterfactual-source-binding.md) also binds the baseline to WHO's registered source definition. The v4 endpoint validator accepts an ID, version and digest rather than an assessor-written baseline description. Relabeling the same quantity as preindustrial harm or complete pollutant removal fails. This preserves the source's comparison claim without asserting that its full model or numeric minimum-risk parameters have been independently reproduced.

The [source-native endpoint validator](../../src/tulip-source-native-burden.js) retains the WHO lower, central and upper DALY values and the report's one-million-DALY resolution. It rejects a changed year, geography, unit, age-standardized rate, cause scope, source or single-year reporting basis. The assessment's copied observation must also match a separately stored [source-observation registry](../../src/tulip-source-observation-registry.js). The registered transcription has a fixed artifact digest, source version and report locator; changing only the candidate packet cannot create a new "source-backed" number. Adding or changing a registered row needs a source-artifact revision and editorial verification of its location, scope and values. Registration is **not** independent scientific review. The validator **only permits endpoint coverage**: a source row cannot self-certify that it covers a whole node. The [fixed-anchor mapping experiment](../../src/tulip-burden-scale-experiment.js) requires matching single-year anchors, then demonstrates a monotone logarithmic interpolation on **synthetic test anchors**: adding an unrelated node leaves a result unchanged, and hypothetical burdens of 100 million versus one billion DALYs remain distinguishable. Its output explicitly says `publicReady: false` and retains the exact anchor definition. The [adversarial tests](../../scripts/test-tulip-source-native-burden.mjs) and [mapping tests](../../scripts/test-tulip-burden-scale-experiment.mjs) validate these mechanical properties, not the scientific or value choices of the anchor magnitudes.

A [modeled allocation experiment](../../src/tulip-burden-conversion-experiment.js) covers a potential second route for a driver or emissions source. Its [v2 source-binding correction](./modeled-allocation-source-binding.md) accepts references to fixed model records for a *modeled fraction of parent attributable DALYs*, rather than fractions or parent quantities entered by a node assessor. It reconstructs the parent from the source registry and checks geography, period, source versions, baseline identity and exact derivation content. An emissions share or exposure share cannot substitute for a harm fraction. The [allocation tests](../../scripts/test-tulip-burden-conversion-experiment.mjs) use an explicitly **synthetic** registered model and test calculation, input changes and future-case invariance. The default registry has no validated real allocations. Model validity and overlap treatment still require source and causal review before numerical use; reported ranges are never assumed to form a probability interval.

For a future-proof health route, the anchor magnitudes must be independently judged against source-checked burden cases and frozen with a method version before this mapper can yield a publishable rating. A [WHO-based human-health anchor candidate](./human-health-anchor-candidate.md) now supplies concrete proposed magnitudes and tests their policy sensitivity; it has **not** passed that adjudication. How health loss compares with services and nature remains a documented TULIP policy decision. The [catalogue route inventory](../../artifacts/tulip-consequence-rule-audit-2026-09-27/route-coverage.json) shows why this health-specific route alone cannot cover all nodes.
