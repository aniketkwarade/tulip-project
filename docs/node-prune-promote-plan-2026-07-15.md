## What To Do

- `Keep as-is`: node can remain visible and conceptually prominent without qualification-heavy copy

- `Demote`: keep in the repo, but reduce discovery prominence and do not present as core canon until evidence improves
- `Research track`: keep available, but treat as an evidence backlog rather than stable canon

## Product Rule

- Keep the graph centered on strong anchors, not on procedural exhaust.
