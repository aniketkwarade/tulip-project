Tulip Earth Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Tulip Earth is an educational climate and environmental systems application. It does not use advertising trackers, sell personal information, or require an account.

Information stored on your device

Tulip Earth may store search history, Analyse navigation history, and footprint answers locally so it can preserve your progress. This information remains on your device and can be removed by deleting the app or clearing its data.

Optional local conditions on iPhone

When you first open the iPhone app, Tulip Earth offers an optional approximate-location choice for local weather context. If you choose to enable it, iOS asks for permission while you use the app. Tulip Earth does not look up your location at that point; it requests your location only when you open Search or refresh local conditions. Search works without location. The app rounds the location to one decimal place (roughly 11 km of latitude) before using Apple's place-name service and requesting current conditions, historical temperature comparisons, and available official weather alerts from Apple WeatherKit. WeatherKit does not supply an air-quality or smoke reading for this feature. Search may suggest external air-quality monitors, water utilities, drought reports, and US river or coastal sources based on the approximate place name. Those sources open separately; Tulip Earth does not automatically send them your coordinates. Tulip Earth does not send your question, search history, footprint answers, or account identity with the WeatherKit request, does not store the coordinate after the current app session, and does not track your location in the background. You can turn local context off within Search or revoke permission in iPhone Settings.

Contact information and messages

The iPhone app opens your email app when you choose Contact. Tulip Earth receives only the information you choose to include after you send the email. The website may separately offer a Contact form. Correspondence is used to respond to your request and maintain necessary records; it is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Scientific source links

Links to scientific sources and registries open outside Tulip Earth. Those sites have their own privacy practices. Tulip Earth does not automatically send your footprint answers, search history, or Analyse history to those sites.

Retention and deletion

On-device information remains until it is cleared by you or the app is deleted. Contact correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary to answer the request, maintain a record of the exchange, or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of contact correspondence by emailing the address below.

Your choices

You can use Explore, Analyse, Activity Impacts, My Footprint, and Search without enabling location. Local conditions and the Contact form are optional.
