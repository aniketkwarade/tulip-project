TULIP Privacy Policy

Effective August 11, 2026

TULIP is an educational climate and environmental systems application. It does not use advertising trackers, sell personal information, or require an account.

Information stored on your device

TULIP may store search history, Analyse navigation history, and footprint answers locally so it can preserve your progress. This information remains on your device and can be removed by deleting the app or clearing its data.

Contact information and messages

The iPhone app opens your email app when you choose Contact. TULIP receives only the information you choose to include after you send the email. The website may separately offer a Contact form. Correspondence is used to respond to your request and maintain necessary records; it is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Scientific source links

Links to scientific sources and registries open outside TULIP. Those sites have their own privacy practices. TULIP does not automatically send your footprint answers, search history, or Analyse history to those sites.

Retention and deletion

On-device information remains until it is cleared by you or the app is deleted. Contact correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary to answer the request, maintain a record of the exchange, or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of contact correspondence by emailing the address below.

Your choices

You can use Explore, Analyse, Activity Impacts, and My Footprint without submitting personal information. The Contact form is optional.
