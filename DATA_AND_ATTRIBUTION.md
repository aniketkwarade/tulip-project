# Data, Sources, and Attribution

TULIP combines project-authored software and analysis with factual records,
derived snapshots, citations, and links originating from many independent
providers.

## What Apache-2.0 covers

The repository's Apache License 2.0 covers the project-authored source code and
documentation to the extent the project has the right to license them. It does
not grant rights to third-party datasets, papers, reports, photographs, logos,
names, trademarks, or other material merely because that material is cited,
linked, described, or stored in this repository.

## Source provenance

The principal machine-readable provenance index is
[`public/tulip-source-registry.json`](public/tulip-source-registry.json). It
records source names, URLs, access modes, public snapshot filenames, evidence
roles, and operational status. Additional domain-specific `*-sources.json`
files and the in-app **Sources** and **Registries** views retain more detailed
locators and transformation notes.

Major provider families represented in the project include United Nations and
specialized agencies, IPCC, NOAA, NASA, USGS, EPA, EIA, the World Bank,
Copernicus and European institutions, national statistical and scientific
agencies, GBIF, OBIS, Our World in Data, universities, journals, and independent
research organizations. Their names identify provenance and do not imply
endorsement.

## Redistribution responsibilities

Before redistributing or commercially reusing a bundled snapshot or source
artifact:

1. Locate its source record and original provider URL.
2. Review the provider's current license, terms of use, attribution language,
   access restrictions, and database-rights rules.
3. Preserve required attribution, notices, methodology, units, time period,
   geographic scope, and modification statements.
4. Do not imply that TULIP, Apache-2.0, or a public download overrides the
   provider's terms.
5. Remove the artifact from a redistribution if the applicable rights cannot
   be established.

Source URLs can change, and this notice is not a substitute for reviewing the
current provider terms.
