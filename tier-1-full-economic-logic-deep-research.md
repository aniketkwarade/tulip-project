# LostPlanet Tier 1 Deep Research

## Objective

Research Tier 1 nodes for **full additive economic logic**.

This tier is for nodes where the product can meaningfully add all five of these fields without forcing weak or repetitive content:

- `Hidden Cost`
- `Who Pays`
- `Physical Limit`
- `Default Driver`
- `System Levers`

## Non-Duplication Rule

This research is **additive only**. Do **not** restate or lightly paraphrase information already covered by the current:

- `Impact on Humans`
- `Impact on Planet`
- domain labels
- affected populations
- primary pathways
- visible direct harms
- visible ecological consequences

Only include material that adds net-new value to one of the five fields above.

## Product Structure To Support

### Impact on Humans

Keep existing content. Add only:

- `Hidden Cost`
- `Who Pays`

### Impact on Planet

Keep existing content. Add only:

- `Physical Limit`

### What Can Be Done

Add:

- `Default Driver`
- `System Levers`

Important:

- `Default Driver` explains what keeps the problem structurally locked in.
- `System Levers` must be structural, not lifestyle advice.

## Output Format Per Node

For each node, return:

### `[node_id]`

- `Research confidence:` `High`, `Medium`, or `Emerging`
- `Hidden Cost:` max 2 sentences
- `Why this is not duplicative:` 1 sentence
- `Who Pays:` max 2 sentences
- `Why this is not duplicative:` 1 sentence
- `Physical Limit:` max 2 sentences
- `Why this is not duplicative:` 1 sentence
- `Default Driver:` max 2 sentences
- `Why this belongs in What Can Be Done:` 1 sentence
- `System Levers:` 3 to 5 flat bullets
- `Key evidence:` 3 to 6 bullets with source, date if available, factual takeaway, URL
